    “Design an interactive iOS mobile game called Cat Cuddle. The game should feature three different kittens: an orange tabby (sarman), a British Shorthair fold (grey British Fold), and a sleek black kitten. All cats must look fluffy, cute, and young (kitten style). The player should be able to choose which kitten they want to cuddle, and the cat’s color and breed should be visually distinct. The game mechanic is simple: when the player taps and swipes on the cat, it reacts with a purring sound (“mrrr”) and starts smiling or showing a happy face. The interaction should feel warm, rewarding, and playful. The cat should have subtle idle animations (tail moving, blinking eyes, breathing softly) to make it feel alive even when untouched. The design should be cozy, cartoon-like but semi-realistic, with soft colors and warm background tones. Create UI elements for cat selection (3 options with icons/pictures), a main interaction screen (full view of the kitten), and gentle feedback animations (heart icons, sparkles) when the cat is being petted.”
